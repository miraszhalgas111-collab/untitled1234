Understanding Finance

Finance helps individuals and businesses manage money, make decisions and plan for the future.

💰 Personal Finance

Personal finance includes budgeting, saving, managing expenses and planning financial goals.

📊 Financial Planning

Financial planning helps people understand their income, expenses and long-term financial needs.

📈 Investment

Investments can help people grow their money over time and create long-term opportunities.

🏢 Corporate Finance

Corporate finance focuses on how companies manage money, investments and business growth.
